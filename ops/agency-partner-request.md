# Agency cooperation and authorised data request

Adapt the greeting and the requested dataset to the agency's actual offering.
This is an unsent template, not a record of a partnership or data permission.

Subject: Authorised data access and agency cooperation — EU Investicijske prilike

Hello [agency] team,

We are developing EU Investicijske prilike to identify a suitable investor for a property your agency represents, or a suitable EU property for an investor whose requirements you represent. We would like to offer these opportunities to your agency so that you can handle the subsequent client contact, brokerage and transaction.

For matching, could you provide authorised access to your property listings and/or documented investor acquisition requirements? Your agency can remain the contact for each record; we do not need the direct contact details of your clients. Please keep an identifiable reference linking each property or requirement to your agency and an available enquiry channel.

For sale properties we need the record ID/URL, country and relevant location, property type, condition, separate building and land areas, asking price/currency where stated, sale/availability status and changes or removals. For buyers we need the target countries/regions, property types, area ranges, condition/redevelopment requirements, additional conditions and budget where stated. Unknown values can remain unknown; company location is not a buyer's acquisition location.

Please confirm the permitted cooperation and data-use scope, including internal storage and matching, short generic summaries without the property's address, precise location or owner's contact details, retention/deletion and attribution. Can enquiries for a specific listing or buyer requirement be handled through your agency?

If available, please share outbound API/feed documentation, an anonymised test sample, authentication requirements, update frequency and changed/deleted-record handling, and pricing/trial terms. Ideally updates would run every six hours, subject to your limits. If no feed exists, please indicate another authorised route.

Our intended remuneration is a fee agreed with and paid by your agency only after a successfully completed sale, subject to applicable requirements and a suitable agreement. Please indicate whether you are open to this cooperation and any restrictions. This enquiry does not accept a paid service, subscription or contract.

Kind regards,
Luka Kadoić

## Recording a reply

- Record the email reference/date and the scope explicitly agreed by the agency.
- An acknowledgement, public agency contact, listing, or technical capability statement is not permission or acceptance of a fee.
- Keep partnership confirmation, data-use permission, fee agreement, listing verification and actual import/refresh evidence separate.
- Do not mark a source active until its actual authorised import and regular refresh have been verified. This template does not implement partner settings or source activation.
